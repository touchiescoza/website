# Product Overview

## Touchies - Touch Rugby Community Platform

Touchies is South Africa's premier touch rugby community platform that connects players, organizes leagues, and promotes touch rugby events across the country.

### Core Purpose
- Connect touch rugby players and teams across South Africa
- Organize and promote touch rugby leagues and tournaments
- Share news and updates from the touch rugby community
- Provide information about upcoming events and training opportunities

### Key Features
- **League Management**: Multiple formats including 6 Down, One Touch, and Beach Touch
- **Event Discovery**: Tournaments, training camps, and social events
- **News Hub**: Latest updates from the South African touch rugby scene
- **Community Building**: Connect players and teams nationwide

### Target Audience
- Touch rugby players of all skill levels
- Teams looking to join leagues
- Event organizers and tournament directors
- Touch rugby enthusiasts and fans

### Brand Identity
- **Primary Color**: Bold green (#00D100) representing energy and sport
- **Tone**: Friendly, inclusive, and community-focused
- **Values**: Accessibility, sportsmanship, and community building
